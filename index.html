Share & merge votes

Every device stores its own votes. To combine everyone's answers on one page:

  1. Each household votes and taps "Copy my vote link".
  2. They send you the link (text / email / WhatsApp).
  3. You paste each link into the address bar and open it — the vote is added automatically.
  4. Then open the page on the TV or share the "all votes" link.
