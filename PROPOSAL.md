# Annual Rafting Trip Proposal — Oregon & Washington 2026

**Prepared for:** Our family & the family-friend couple
**Goal:** Find the best place + an available weekend everyone can agree on.

Summer 2026 is booked up fast, so this proposal shortlists **4 of the best and most interesting rafting trips** in Oregon and Washington. They are deliberately different — a quick classic, a waterfall showpiece, a wilderness getaway, and a September "secret season" trip — so we have something to negotiate with for every kind of schedule.

> **How we decide:** everyone votes on the website, picks the weekends they're available, and the site shows us the dates where *everyone* is free plus the group's favorite destination.

---

## The 4 Shortlisted Trips

### 1. Lower Deschutes River — Maupin, Oregon ⭐ The Oregon Classic
- **Class:** III–IV (fun, splashy, beginner-friendly options)
- **Format:** Half-day (13 mi), Full-day (13–15 mi, lunch), or 2–5 day overnight camping
- **Season:** April – October (dam-controlled, so it keeps good water all summer — even late September)
- **Price (full-day):** ~$105–$139/adult, kids ~$95–$129 · Overnight 2–3 days from ~$450+
- **Age:** 6+ (kids as young as 4 on some outfitters)
- **Drive:** ~2 hr from Portland, <2 hr from Bend
- **Why it's interesting:** Oregon's most-rafted river. A 2,600-ft basalt canyon, big splashy rapids like **Whitehorse, Wapinitia, Boxcar**, swimming/rock-jumping stops, bighorn sheep and osprey. The most reliable, flexible option of the four — easiest to slot into a tight calendar.

### 2. White Salmon River — Columbia River Gorge, WA 🌊 Best in the West
- **Class:** III–IV
- **Format:** Half-day (8 mi, ~2 hr) or Full-day (15 mi, ~4 hr, riverside lunch)
- **Season:** June – September (Husum Falls most runnable in August)
- **Price:** Half-day ~$88–$130 · Full-day ~$150–$170
- **Age:** 7–10+
- **Drive:** ~90 min from Portland (near Hood River)
- **Why it's interesting:** Glacial meltwater off Mt. Adams through a wild, undammed gorge. The headline is **Husum Falls — a 12–14 ft drop, the tallest commercially rafted waterfall in the U.S.** (optional: run it or walk around). Rapids like The Cave, Corkscrew, and Rattlesnake. One of the most memorable single days on water in the PNW.

### 3. Rogue River (Wild & Scenic Section) — Grants Pass, Oregon 🏕️ The Wilderness Getaway
- **Class:** II–IV (mostly II–III; one big one: Blossom Bar, Class IV)
- **Format:** 3–5 day guided camping or lodge trips (35–42 miles)
- **Season:** May – September
- **Price:** From ~$1,185–$1,599/person all-inclusive (meals, gear, guides, camp)
- **Age:** 7+
- **Drive:** ~4.5–5 hr from Portland; near Grants Pass
- **Why it's interesting:** The classic "week in the woods" — wildlife (bald eagles, otters, black bears), warm water (high 60s–70s°F), side hikes to historic mining cabins, hot springs, and the famous Rogue River Trail. No cell service, stars everywhere. The best choice if we want a real multi-day escape rather than a day trip.

### 4. Tieton River — near Naches/Yakima, Washington 🍂 The September Secret
- **Class:** III–IV (nonstop)
- **Format:** Day trip, 14–15 miles, ~4 hr
- **Season:** **September only** — the famous "Flip-Flop" dam release from Rimrock Lake
- **Price:** ~$85–$117/adult
- **Age:** 10–12+
- **Drive:** ~2.75 hr from Seattle; ~1 hr from Mt. Rainier
- **Why it's interesting:** The rarest trip of the year. A quiet creek all summer that turns into Washington's steepest, fastest whitewater for a few weeks each September — **warmest water in the state**, fall colors, and it's the finale of the whole PNW rafting season. Perfect if every summer weekend is already taken: it opens up a brand-new block of dates nobody else has booked yet.

## Suggested Negotiation Strategy

- **If we want a guaranteed classic with the least scheduling pain:** Lower Deschutes (holds water through fall, many outfitters, day *or* overnight).
- **If we want the single most memorable day:** White Salmon (Husum Falls).
- **If we want a real vacation with the friend couple:** Rogue multi-day.
- **If every remaining summer weekend is booked:** Tieton in September — brand-new dates most people haven't claimed.

## Suggested Candidate Dates (for the availability poll)

All weekends from mid-August through late September 2026 (Aug 8 → Sep 27), so the Tieton (Sept-only) and the summer trips can all be compared side by side. Add a few weekday options for anyone with flexible schedules.
